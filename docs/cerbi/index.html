Cerbi: documents

Cerbi is the AI document automation platform I built at Titan Aviation Fuels. It reads scanned and handwritten freight paperwork, files what is complete and holds the rest for a person with a short reason. These pages are the portfolio versions of its documents: carrier, customer, coworker and internal system names are left out; the architecture, decisions and numbers are real.
