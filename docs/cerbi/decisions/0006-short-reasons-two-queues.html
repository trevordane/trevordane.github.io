ADR 0006: Short hold reasons, and two hold queues

Status: accepted · Date: 5–6 Oct 2026

Context

Held loads carried long, value-filled reasons, so no two read the same and the hold list could not be grouped or filtered. And "held" covered two different situations: loads a person had to decide, and paperwork that was simply early (a BOL before its invoice).

Decision

  • Every rule that holds, re-reads or discards carries a short reason: at most six words, no values, the same words for every load it holds. The numbers and what to do go in a separate note.
  • Two queues: Purgatory for loads a person must decide, and Limbo for loads waiting on paperwork, which re-run themselves when it arrives.
  • Recheck re-runs every rule against fresh reference data without a paid re-read, keeping anything a person typed.

Consequences

  • Purgatory reads as a grouped to-do list: a count under each short reason.
  • Counting every load since launch, not one month, Purgatory fell from 4,645 loads (12.6%) on 6 Oct to 1,358 (2.7%) on 30 Oct, while total loads grew 35%.
  • In October about 4 in 10 holds were cleared by Cerbi itself and about 4 in 10 were replaced by a fresh read; about 1 in 10 needed a person.
  • Limbo has to be watched for paperwork that never comes; an aged-holds report covers that.
